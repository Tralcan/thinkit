Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how this application ("the App") handles information and protects your privacy. Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to collecting as little information as possible while providing the functionality of the App.

Overview

The App is designed with privacy as a core principle. Unless explicitly stated within the App, it does not require users to create accounts and does not collect personal information for advertising, marketing, profiling, or analytics purposes.

Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, the App may process certain information necessary to provide its functionality. Whenever possible, this processing occurs directly on your device.

Unless required for a specific feature, the App does not collect or store:

  • Name, address, or contact information
  • Location data
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Behavioral analytics
  • Marketing profiles
  • Personal content stored on external servers operated by us

On-Device Processing

Many features of the App perform processing directly on your Apple device using Apple's built-in technologies whenever available. This minimizes the amount of information that leaves your device.

Artificial Intelligence Services

Some features may optionally use trusted third-party Artificial Intelligence (AI) services to provide functionality requested by the user, such as generating summaries, organizing information, answering questions, creating content, or providing intelligent suggestions.

Only the information necessary to provide the requested feature is transmitted to these AI providers. The information is not used by us for advertising, profiling, or marketing purposes.

Subscriptions and Purchases

If the App offers premium features, subscriptions and purchases are managed entirely through the Apple App Store.

We do not receive, process, or store payment card information. Payment information is handled by Apple according to Apple's privacy and payment policies.

Data Storage

User-generated content may be stored locally on the user's device. If enabled by the user, certain data may also synchronize through Apple services such as iCloud.

Unless specifically required by the App's functionality, we do not maintain our own database of personal user information.

Third-Party Services

The App may rely on trusted third-party service providers to deliver certain features, including Artificial Intelligence services, cloud synchronization, or Apple platform services.

These services are used solely to provide the functionality requested by the user and are not used for advertising or user tracking by us.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page along with the updated revision date.
